How it works: every generator consumes a plain data spec (TSV columns for Urdu text, Roman transliteration, English meaning, and optional highlight tokens) and emits a finished, playable video or card set. Adding a lesson is editing a row — the layout, shaping, highlighting, and encoding are handled by the skill.
