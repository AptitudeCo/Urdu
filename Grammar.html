1

Simple Present Tense

3:07

  • Basic sentence order is subject, time, object, main verb, then helping verb.
  • Time is not always needed — leave it out when there is no specific time.
  • Verb endings change with the speaker's gender: a male speaker says پڑھتا ہوں, a female speaker says پڑھتی ہوں.

Watch for: Gender agreement on the main verb (ta / ti).

2

Present Continuous Tense

2:51

  • Same subject / time / object / verb frame, plus a helping verb (am, is, are).
  • raha, rahi, rahe attach to the verb: raha with a singular male subject, rahi with a singular female subject.
  • rahe with plural subjects.

Watch for: Choosing raha / rahi / rahe, and matching the helping verb.

3

Simple Past Tense

2:16

  • Used for something that happened in the past.
  • Pattern: ne after the subject, then the object, then the past form of the verb — usne kam kiya, hamne khana khaya.

Watch for: Placing ne immediately after the subject.

4

Past Continuous Tense

2:29

  • Used for something that was happening at a certain time in the past.
  • raha / rahi / rahe go on the verb, together with a past helping verb — tha, thi, the.

Watch for: raha / rahi / rahe plus tha / thi / the.

Simple Future Tense

2:10

  • Used for something that will happen in the future.
  • The verb ending changes depending on the gender and number of the subject — endings like unga, egi, enge.

Watch for: The verb ending follows the gender and number of the subject.

Future Continuous Tense
